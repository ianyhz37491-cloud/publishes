# Serenity — recorded sound sources

Serenity uses **real field recordings** instead of synthesized broad-band noise.

All sounds below are identified as CC0/public-domain-equivalent by their source repositories. The files are fetched at pinned commits during the Pages build and verified against their expected Git blob IDs before publication.

- **fields.ogg**, perennial grass/rustling wheat breeze: [Dewdrop fields ambience](https://github.com/Lelio88/dewdrop/blob/main/assets/audio/fields_amb.ogg), originally *Wheat in the Wind* by bdvictor, [Freesound #240914](https://freesound.org/s/240914/) (CC0), as documented by [Dewdrop credits](https://github.com/Lelio88/dewdrop/blob/main/CREDITS.md).
- **meadow.m4a**, meadow insects and faint field ambience: [Garuda1982, Freesound #529573](https://freesound.org/people/Garuda1982/sounds/529573/), CC0, via [Ambiently](https://github.com/abhinandansharma/ambiently).
- **forest.m4a**, natural early-morning birds and forest ambience: [J.Zazvurek, Freesound #353311](https://freesound.org/people/J.Zazvurek/sounds/353311/), CC0, via Ambiently.
- **night.m4a**, nocturnal crickets/cicadas: [kyles, Freesound #453862](https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/453862/), CC0, via Ambiently.

Audio has no autoplay: the visitor enables it deliberately. Its four continuous layers crossfade with the solar mood cycle; the rustling field layer gains subtle local emphasis when the cursor brushes the grass.

No music, microphone recordings, AI voices, or synthesized noise are used. The sources are deliberately credited here even though CC0 does not require attribution.
